# Script2Prompt — Visual Identity

Brand direction for the project logo, type, and color. Design goals: minimal,
flat-vector, readable at app-icon sizes, and clearly about *converting a script
into a video prompt*.

## Logo concept

A text-file icon and a video-file icon, joined by an arrow that signifies the
conversion of one into the other.

## Image-generation prompt

> A clean, minimal flat-vector app icon: a text-file icon on the left and a
> video-file icon on the right, joined by a bold right-pointing arrow that
> suggests converting the script into a video prompt. Simple geometric shapes,
> high contrast, limited palette (one accent color plus dark gray/white),
> centered composition with generous padding and a transparent or solid
> background so it reads clearly at small sizes. No text, no lettering, no
> photorealism — crisp edges and a modern software-logo aesthetic.

## Typography

A modern geometric sans for the wordmark/UI, with a monospace accent as a
thematic nod to screenplays and code.

| Role | Font | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark / headings | **Space Grotesk** | Slightly technical, distinctive; matches the crisp icon. |
| UI / body | **Inter** | Neutral, highly legible, pairs cleanly with Grotesk. |
| Monospace accent (versions, tags) | **JetBrains Mono** or **IBM Plex Mono** | Coding/technical feel for small labels. |

All three are open source, which fits the project's license and zero-dependency
ethos.

Alternates:

- *Sora* / *Outfit* — softer, more geometric.
- *IBM Plex Sans* — more corporate/technical.

Avoid cursive or decorative faces — they fight the minimal icon.

## Color

- Keep it to **one accent color** plus a neutral dark gray and white.
- Ensure sufficient contrast on both light and dark backgrounds for small-size
  legibility (favicon, app icon).
