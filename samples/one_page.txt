INT. NEWSROOM - NIGHT

Fluorescent lights hum over rows of empty desks. ELENA (40s, sharp editor, coiled energy) stands at the glass wall, phone pressed to her ear.

ELENA
(into phone)
Print it. I don't care who it burns.

She hangs up. Across the room a junior reporter, DANI (20s, nervous), clutches a notepad.

DANI
They'll fire you for this.

ELENA
Then I'll sleep in.

The elevator dings. A security guard enters, sweeping a flashlight across the floor.
